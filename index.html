What We Do

Club Activities & Programs

Explore the regular tournaments, fitness regimes, charity projects, and networking fixtures of the Aba Bankers FC.

🏆

Aba Inter-Bank Football League

Our marquee annual championship pitting bank branches across Factory Road, Faulks Road, Azikiwe Road, and Market Road against each other in a round-robin thriller for the Bankers Gold Cup.

📅 Annual Season (Sept – Nov) ⚽ 14 Banks Competing
🏃‍♂️

Saturday Morning Fitness & Drills

Weekly physical conditioning, tactical formations, and scrimmage matches every Saturday morning. Dedicated to keeping desk-bound corporate bankers agile, energized, and healthy.

📅 Every Saturday 6:30 AM 📍 Enyimba Stadium Practice Turf
🤝

Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

Giving back to the Aba community through youth football clinics in Ariaria, footwear and soccer ball donations to grassroots academies, and charity health walks.

📅 Quarterly Outreach ❤️ Aba South & North Communities
🌐

Inter-City Bankers Derby

High-voltage friendly and tournament ties against sister bankers football teams from Port Harcourt, Owerri, Uyo, and Enugu, expanding business networks beyond Abia State.

📅 Bi-Annual Invitational 📍 Regional Stadiums
👔

Veterans & Executives Novelty Cup

Special quarter-yearly friendly fixtures designed for senior bank managers, branch heads, and retired bankers to enjoy the beautiful game at an executive, friendly pace.

📅 Quarterly Sundays 📍 Aba Sports Club Ground
🥂

Annual Bankers Awards & Gala Night

The red-carpet celebration honoring the Golden Boot winner, Most Valuable Player, Best Goalkeeper, Bank of the Year, and official induction of newly registered players.

📅 December Grand Finale 🎖️ Trophies & Medals
